## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:**
- The parts that belong to this product are the tokens, the charts and three moments: the look-through treemap, the Wall view and the Chat proposal card.
- Everything around them could belong to any product. Six screens share the same frame: an uppercase micro-label, a huge number, a chip row, then a stack of identical bordered cards in a centred 820px column. Home adds four stat tiles that restate figures already shown twice above them.
- Swap brass for blue and this passes for any fintech SaaS template. Only the Wall view, with no cards and no boxed labels, reads as the personal instrument PRODUCT.md describes.

**Deterministic scan:**
- **Source scan:** 1 finding, `side-tab` at `ui/src/screens/LookThrough.svelte:161`. It's an inset 2px stripe marking the selected table row, so it's a selection marker and not decoration. That makes it a soft false positive.
- **Rendered scan:** 9 views × 2 themes, 179 findings in dark and 215 in light. Contrast dominates:
  - `--text-3` on a card measures 3.0:1 (dark) and 2.8:1 (light), 70 instances per theme.
  - `--text-3` on the page measures 3.2:1 and 2.6:1, 39 instances per theme.
  - Treemap percentages measure 2.3–2.9:1, and the selected cell name in light measures 1.7:1.
  - Light `--positive` measures 3.8–4.4:1, and light `--accent` links measure 4.0:1.
  - There are 34 instances of 11px `tiny-text`, plus 7 tags on Changes at 10px.
- **Where the detector agrees with the review:** both flagged the `--text-3` contrast.
- **What only the detector caught:** the treemap label contrast, the 1.7:1 selected name, and the 10px Changes tags.
- **Style flags:** `overused-font` (Instrument Sans) and `cream-palette` (light `#f3efe6` ground) are style flags rather than defects. They do matter for the goal of feeling less AI-made, because the warm cream page with a grotesque sans is a common generated look right now.
- **Skipped:** the detector's URL mode timed out on every view, because the server-sent event stream keeps the page busy. The overlay injection ran instead.

## Overall Impression

The data design is ahead of the page design. The charts, colours and figures have a point of view. The scaffolding around them (cards, micro-labels, KPI tiles, pills) is stock. The biggest opportunity is to make Home answer "why did it move" in place of the repeated tiles, and to drop the card-on-card chrome in favour of a ruled-ledger structure.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The privacy blur doesn't blur anything**
- **Where:** `ui/src/App.svelte:41` sets `<svelte:body class:blur={prefs.blur} />`.
- **Verified:** with `ww.blur=true`, `body.className` is `""` and the computed filter on `.money` is `none`. The rule `.blur .money` in `ui/src/tokens.css:61` never matches. Only the chart (`.plot.blur`) and the Wall blur.
- **Why it matters:** it breaks the privacy commitment and reports itself as active.
- **Fix:** toggle the class on `document.documentElement` in an `$effect`, add a visible "amounts hidden" state, and add an e2e test.
- **Command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Home restates itself and leads with a misleading red figure**
- **What:**
  - Group totals appear three times: the bar legend, the tiles at `ui/src/screens/Home.svelte:94-99`, and the group lists.
  - The Liquid net worth tile (`Home.svelte:18`) shows −$145,123 in red under a rising headline.
  - The stale banner sits above the hero and says "45 days" for an account that is 187 days stale.
- **Fix:**
  - Remove the tiles.
  - Put a compact market / added / debt / FX split since the last check-in under the hero.
  - Redefine liquid net worth or drop it.
  - Turn the banner into one quiet line that names the account.
- **Command:** `/impeccable distill`

**[P1] Contrast fails on faint text, the treemap and the Overlap matrix**
- **What:**
  - `--text-3` (`ui/src/tokens.css:11`, light at `:42` and `:50`) measures 2.6–3.2:1 on about 110 elements per theme.
  - Treemap percentages measure 2.3–2.9:1.
  - The Overlap shading (`ui/src/screens/Overlap.svelte:31-34`) puts dark text on dark-grey cells in light mode.
- **Fix:**
  - Raise `--text-3` to at least 4.5:1 in both themes.
  - Flip treemap and overlap label colour by cell luminance.
  - Shade Overlap with a single sequential ramp and show only the upper triangle.
- **Command:** `/impeccable colorize`, then `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] Navigation is hard to reach on mobile, on long pages and from the keyboard**
- **What:**
  - The rail isn't sticky, so its utility links sit around y=1300 (`ui/src/lib/components/Rail.svelte:20-28`).
  - On mobile it becomes a sideways scroller (`Rail.svelte:47-52`) that shows only three items.
  - There is no skip link, and Home has no `h1`.
- **Fix:**
  - Make the rail sticky and fold its utilities into ⌘K.
  - Add a mobile bottom bar.
  - Add a skip link and an `h1` on every screen.
- **Command:** `/impeccable adapt`

**[P2] Changes buries its own answer**
- **What:**
  - Six equal panels in a fixed-height board, each scrolling inside the page (`ui/src/screens/Changes.svelte` `.board` / `.body`).
  - The bridge is a small panel.
  - "VTIQuestrade RRSP" renders with no gap.
  - Tags are set at 10px.
- **Fix:**
  - Make the bridge the full-width hero.
  - Show panels at natural height, with disclosure for drift and fund changes.
  - Cut the period presets to four.
- **Command:** `/impeccable layout`

## Questions to Consider

- Home's job is "what am I worth, is it going up, why". Why does the "why" live on another screen?
- The Wall is the only screen that feels like an instrument. What if Home were the Wall with a ledger under it?
- What does brass mean? Today it means six things.
- Does every section need a box? What would ruled hairlines on paper look like?
- Budget is secondary, yet it is one of the most elaborate screens and holds a primary rail slot. Should it?
